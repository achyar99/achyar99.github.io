Research / Bismuth perovskite SNN
Project

Memristor-aware spiking networks on bismuth-halide perovskite devices

A lead-free perovskite memristor model, and a spiking network trained to tolerate that device's variability.

~92.5%memristor-aware SNN, no variability
σ = 0.25variability where naive training falls furthest behind
Neural networksPaper replicationsRRAM

The problem

A spiking network trained on ideal weights loses accuracy when deployed on variable devices. Training with the device model in the loop should make it more robust.

What I did

  • Built compact models of iodine- and bromine-rich bismuth-halide perovskite memristors from published I–V data (Kim et al.).
  • Wrote a differentiable crossbar layer with finite conductance levels and programming noise.
  • Trained spiking networks with surrogate gradients, naive versus memristor-aware, and swept device variability.

What I learned

Memristor-aware training kept accuracy higher across the whole variability range.
