Research / PCH memory, device to chip
Project

Phase-change heterostructure memory, from device to chip

A device-to-chip simulation pipeline for our group's multi-level phase-change heterostructure (PCH) memories.

Scope
Multi-levelcells mapped to signed network weights
VGG8device-aware training on CIFAR-10
NeuroSimchip-level throughput and efficiency estimate
Phase-change memoryNeural networks

The question

Our group fabricates phase-change heterostructure cells that store several resistance levels with low power. Device papers report resistance, drift and energy, but not what the cells mean for an AI chip.

What I did

  • Digitised level resistances, cycle-to-cycle spread and read noise from the published device figures, keeping every value traceable to its source.
  • Built a crossbar model where each weight is a differential pair of discrete levels, including drift and global drift compensation.
  • Ran device-aware VGG8 training on CIFAR-10 and studied IR drop, ADC precision, programming energy and variability.
  • Estimated chip-level throughput and efficiency with NeuroSim and compared the devices with a GST baseline.

Results available on request

Results and figures from this project are not shared publicly yet. Happy to talk about the work in person or by email.
